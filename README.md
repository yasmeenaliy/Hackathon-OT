# OTrust

Passive OT Asset Discovery Through Fingerprint Similarity and Digital-Twin-Assisted Validation.

## Overview
OTrust was developed during the Operational Technology Cybersecurity Hackathon as a passive-first approach to safer reconnaissance in legacy OT environments.

## The Challenge
Legacy OT assets can be difficult to identify without introducing unnecessary interaction or operational risk.

## Our Approach
OTrust combines:
- Passive behavioral fingerprinting
- Explainable fingerprint similarity
- Ranked asset identity candidates
- Simulated virtual preflight for exceptional verification
- Human-governed decision making

## Prototype
The hackathon prototype demonstrates the OTrust workflow using synthetic data and simulated OT scenarios.

## Important Note
This repository is a hackathon prototype. Demo data, similarity parameters, and simulation results are not production-calibrated and should not be interpreted as validated OT safety or accuracy claims.
